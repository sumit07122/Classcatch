Standard College Community Guidelines
1. Code of Academic Conduct

ClassCatch is a collaborative peer learning environment. Users agree to share genuine, helpful class notes, accurate announcements, and fair feedback.

2. Prohibited Behavior

Users are strictly prohibited from posting hate speech, exam paper leaks during live tests, non-academic advertisements, or harassment in the Campus Lounge or Unofficial Class Chats.

3. Verification & Accuracy

While summaries may receive a "Verified by CR / Faculty" seal, ClassCatch serves as an informal peer resource. Students should always cross-reference official university notices for official administrative decisions.
