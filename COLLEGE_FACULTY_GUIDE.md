# ClassCatch — Institutional Academic Continuity Platform
## Faculty & Institutional Leadership Guide

> **Prepared for:** Deans, Heads of Department (HODs), Course Instructors, and Academic Mentors  
> **Institution:** GLA University, Mathura Campus  
> **Platform Version:** 2.0 (Release Edition)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Every semester, academic departments face a persistent friction: **unplanned student absenteeism**. Whether caused by health emergencies, family commitments, or overlapping placement drives, when students miss lectures, the resulting gaps cause:
1. **Repetitive Instructor Inquiries:** Faculty spend valuable office hours answering *"What did I miss on Tuesday?"*
2. **Scattered, Inaccurate Information:** Informal WhatsApp class groups circulate fragmented notes, incomplete homework deadlines, and incorrect room numbers.
3. **Attendance Falloff:** Students unaware of their precise percentage fall below the mandatory 75% cutoff and face debarment from semester examinations.

**ClassCatch** is an institutional digital hub engineered to solve these exact problems. It provides a structured, moderated ecosystem where absent students can catch up immediately using **Class Representative (CR) verified notes**, live timetable tracking, and official assignment deadline countdowns.

---

## 2. Key Academic Modules & Solutions

### A. The "What Did I Miss?" Absence Engine
- **The Problem:** When an absent student returns to campus, identifying what was covered across 4 or 5 different subjects requires reaching out to multiple classmates across disparate chat threads.
- **The Solution:** The **Catch-Up Hub** (`/catchup`) aggregates everything that occurred on any given lecture date:
  - Concise lecture summaries and formulas covered.
  - Assignments or homework problems assigned.
  - Upcoming quizzes or midterm announcements.

### B. The Class Representative (CR) & Faculty Quality Gate
- **Zero Spam & Anti-Misinformation Guarantee:** Unlike open social media or messaging platforms, student-submitted summaries **do not appear publicly** right away.
- **Verification Workflow:**
  1. A student submits notes for a class lecture.
  2. The submission is held in a **Pending Review** status.
  3. The designated **Class Representative (CR)** or **Faculty Instructor** reviews the summary for accuracy.
  4. Upon verification, the note is stamped with an official **"CR Verified" seal** and published to the class feed.
  5. The student author receives **Karma (+20)** rewarding academic contribution.
- **Content Moderation:** Inaccurate or outdated notes can be removed immediately by the author, the assigned CR, or Department Administrators.

### C. Smart Attendance & 75% Deficit Safeguard
- In compliance with institutional regulations, students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance to qualify for examinations.
- ClassCatch features a built-in mathematical calculator:
  - Calculates the exact number of consecutive classes a student must attend to recover from an attendance dip back to 75%.
  - Alerts students before they enter the danger zone, reducing semester-end detention cases.

### D. Section Privacy & Section Scoping
- Students only access courses and notices for their enrolled section (e.g., Section 2FE).
- Ensures that room changes, timetables, and assignments intended for one specific class do not create confusion in other sections.

### E. Official Class Notice Board & Academic Deadlines
- Important deadlines (lab reports, mini-projects, quiz dates) are logged centrally with color-coded countdown badges (*"2 days left"*, *"Urgent"*).
- Reduces the excuse of *"I didn't know the assignment was due today"*.

## 4. Privacy, Security & Institutional Safeguards

1. **Domain Isolation:** Only users authenticating with verified `@gla.ac.in` email credentials can join the network.
2. **Section Protection:** Students are assigned to specific academic sections; lateral entry batches and standard batches remain orderly and isolated.
3. **Auditability:** All administrative actions, approvals, and content deletions are recorded with timestamps and operator identity in immutable audit logs.
4. **No Commercial Distractions:** Zero advertisements, zero third-party tracking, and completely ad-free academic environment.

---

## 5. Summary Recommendation

ClassCatch transforms student absenteeism from a disruptive academic setback into an organized, peer-supported, and faculty-aligned workflow. It empowers Class Representatives to exercise responsible academic leadership while saving faculty time and ensuring no student gets left behind.
